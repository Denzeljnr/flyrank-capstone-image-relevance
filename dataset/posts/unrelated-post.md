# The Basics of Home Espresso Brewing

Getting good espresso at home starts with fresh beans and consistent
grinding. A burr grinder is essential — blade grinders produce uneven
particles that lead to inconsistent extraction. Aim to use beans within
two to three weeks of their roast date for the best flavor.

Water temperature matters more than most beginners expect. Too hot and
you'll extract bitter compounds; too cool and the shot will taste sour
and underdeveloped. Most machines target somewhere around 90-96°C.

Tamping evenly is a small habit that makes a large difference. An uneven
tamp creates channels in the puck where water rushes through
unpredictably, pulling an inconsistent shot every time.
